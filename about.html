About the Department

Learn about our vision, mission, and the story of the School of Computer Applications.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in computing education, fostering innovation, research, and ethical leadership to solve real-world problems.

Our Mission

  • To impart high-quality technical education through a modern, industry-aligned curriculum.
  • To cultivate a spirit of research and lifelong learning among students and faculty.
  • To instill professional ethics and social responsibility in future software professionals.

Department Overview

Established in 2001, the School of Computer Applications (SCA) at Lovely Professional University has grown to become one of the most sought-after departments for computing education. We offer industry-focused programs like BCA, MCA, and specialized degrees. Our state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong industry partnerships ensure our students are ready for the challenges of the IT industry.
