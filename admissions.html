Programs & Eligibility

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters) | Seats: 300

Eligibility: Pass with 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 or equivalent in any stream. (5% relaxation for reserved categories).

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters) | Seats: 120

Eligibility: Pass with 60% aggregate marks in BCA / B.Sc (CS) / Bachelor's degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or Graduation level.
